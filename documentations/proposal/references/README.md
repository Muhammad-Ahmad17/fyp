## Key numbers used on the slides

- [2] YOLOv3; Pictor-v3 (~1,500 images); best approach 72.3% mAP at 11 FPS on a laptop.
- [3] Eight YOLO v3/v4/v5 models; CHV dataset (1,330 images, 6 classes); YOLOv5x 86.55% mAP, YOLOv5s 52 FPS on a Tesla P40 GPU.
- [7] YOLOv4 fire/smoke detector with filter pruning; D-Fire dataset (21,000+ images); up to 83.60% less compute and 83.86% less memory; Raspberry Pi 4 case study.
- [1] About 2.93 million work-related deaths (2019) and over 395 million non-fatal injuries per year.
